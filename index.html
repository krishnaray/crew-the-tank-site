Project concept

Physical immersive tank simulator

A museum-quality full-scale tank replica combined with an interactive 2-person tank battle experience.

The tank stays the hero. Everything else is a layer we add to it.

  • Physically accurate tank replica as the primary attraction
  • Visitors explore the exterior and the confined crew interior
  • Detailed tracks, wheels, cupolas, hatches, ropes, curvature and equipment
  • Internal shell rack with inert simulated AP / HEAT / HESH rounds
  • External display rack for inert tank equipment and battlefield props
  • Strong metal frame + metal sheet + fibreglass/GFRC finishing
  • Self-supporting, standing on its own wheels and tracks
  • Movable access stairs for top and exterior viewing
  • Durable, maintainable, museum/exhibition-grade construction

Two modes

One installation, two identities

A museum exhibit all day. A battle experience whenever a crew steps in.

Museum Mode · default

The exhibit

  • Walk around, study tracks, hatches and cupolas
  • Climb the stairs and look inside
  • Lift a training round from the shell rack
  • No motion, no game, fully guided
Battle Mode · on demand

The experience

  • Crew helmets with built-in radio
  • Driver drives, gunner aims and fires
  • Incoming fire, mines, near misses
  • The crowd outside watches the battle

Why it works

The hard questions, answered

Every risk in a project like this has a known engineering answer, designed in from day one.

Will it look real?

Rugged steel frame, moulded GFRC/fibreglass body, 3D-printed fine details, and a dedicated team of senior artists for texture, weathering and markings.

Can it carry people?

A welded steel chassis carries all loads through fixed axles to the tracks. Designed for 10 people with a safety factor of 2, engineer signed off.

Is it safe inside?

Only low-voltage DC in the crew space. Mains stays outside. Emergency stops at every seat and outside. Motion small and mechanically limited.

Will it feel like a tank?

No monitors, keyboards or gaming gear in sight. Displays sit behind periscope optics; every control is a tank control.

Can it be maintained?

Custom motion platforms and standard industrial parts, serviced locally by our team. Hinged panels behind every interior face.

Can it move later?

Modular: hull, turret, barrel and skirts separate. Hidden jacking and lifting points let a team of about 10 relocate it.

Our safety commitments

Safety is designed, not added

ElectricalLow-voltage DC only inside. All mains equipment in a separate external enclosure.
Emergency stopAt driver, gunner, operator and outside. Cuts motion power instantly; the game goes to a safe state.
MotionAbout ±5°, three layers of limits ending in steel hard stops. Guards over every moving gap.
StructureEngineer-certified load design and floor check before fabrication starts.
Fire & airFire-retardant materials, smoke/heat sensing, hidden ventilation fans.
SupervisionThe operator watches the crew on interior cameras for the whole session.
